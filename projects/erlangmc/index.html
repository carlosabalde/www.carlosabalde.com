Erlang Memcached Client archived erlang memcached

An Erlang/OTP library wrapping libmemcached to connect to Memcached servers from Erlang applications.

Before 2010, thanks to Victor Gulias (my PhD thesis advisor), Erlang/OTP was my go-to language and platform for roughly a decade starting around 2000. This was pure experimentation around 2008, when I first got hooked on high scalability and everything related to it. Memcached was definitely the hot thing back then.
